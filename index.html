About Free Events

Free Events is an open cultural community bringing people together through theatre, performing arts, creative expression and cultural activities.

Whether you are an artist, performer, volunteer, enthusiast or simply someone looking for meaningful experiences, there is a place for you here.

Come as an audience. Stay as a part of the community.
